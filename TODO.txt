NOTES FOR FUTURE EDITS
====================================================================
- tauri's beforeBuildCommand runs with a cwd that depends on where you
  invoked `tauri` from — that is why the command is a fallback chain
  (ui | ../ui | umbra/ui). Do not simplify it to a single path.
- plain `cargo build` does NOT run beforeBuildCommand; the cross script
  builds umbra/ui itself first.
- www/ is deliberately untracked; never `git add www/`.
- macOS remains intentionally unsupported (no ARM/macOS artifacts).
